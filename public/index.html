About visit analytics

When enabled, we use Vercel Web Analytics to measure visits, QR placement codes, category choices, and provider or map clicks. These reports do not tell us your name or email. We do not request your precise location, and booking takes place on the provider’s website. Our analytics integration respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.
